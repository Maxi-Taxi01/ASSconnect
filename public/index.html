About ASSconnect

A practical bridge between Applied Sciences students and professional opportunities.

What ASSconnect does

ASSconnect helps students make their skills visible and helps professionals find motivated candidates for internships, graduation projects, research collaborations, career opportunities and jobs.

1Students create a concise profile with study phase, availability and focus.
2Professionals search profiles, save candidates and post opportunities.
3ASSconnect keeps moderation, consent and contact requests in one shared place.

Student story

From lab interest to internship shortlist

A Life Science student adds microscopy, Python and summer availability to their profile. A research partner filters by those skills, saves the profile and sends a focused contact request instead of a generic vacancy link.

Graduation project story

A clearer route to a thesis match

An Applied Physics student looking for a graduation project shares a short bio and preferred hybrid schedule. A company posts a sensing project, the profile appears in search, and both sides can start the conversation with the same project context.

Professional story

One place for company visibility

A professional creates a company profile, lists sectors such as biotech and energy, and posts opportunities for approval. Students can discover the company while professionals keep a shortlist of promising profiles.
