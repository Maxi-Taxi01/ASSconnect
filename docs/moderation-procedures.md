# ASSconnect Moderation Procedures (Draft)

**Status: draft for review.** Operational guidance for administrators handling
submissions, reports, appeals and abuse. Adapt to the operating organisation's
policies before launch.

_Last updated: 19 June 2026._

## Tools available to administrators

The admin Operations panel provides a moderation queue (pending student profiles,
pending company profiles and pending opportunities), an accounts list with
suspend/reactivate controls, a searchable audit log, backup creation and restore,
and review of user blocks. Student and company records can be filtered, edited and
removed; reports and privacy requests are reviewable through their endpoints.

## Approving submissions

New and edited student profiles, company profiles and opportunities are held as
**pending** and are not public until approved. Review each submission for
accuracy, appropriateness and policy compliance. Approve genuine submissions;
reject those that are misleading, unlawful or abusive, recording a short
moderation note where supported. Aim to review the queue at least once per
business day.

## Handling reports

Users can report profiles and messages. Review each open report promptly, decide
whether content breaches the terms, and take proportionate action: leave in place,
hide or remove the content, warn the user, or suspend the account. Mark the report
resolved or dismissed with a brief reason.

## Abusive accounts and messages

Recipients can block senders, which stops further contact. Administrators can
review blocks and reported messages. For serious or repeated abuse, suspend the
account (which ends its sessions and blocks login) and, where warranted, remove it
permanently. Record the reason for suspension.

## Suspension, reactivation and removal

Suspend accounts that breach the terms or present a safety/security risk;
reactivate if the issue is resolved or the suspension was in error. Administrator
accounts cannot be suspended through the panel and no one can suspend their own
account. Permanent removal follows the account-deletion path, which anonymises the
account and removes profiles from public view.

## Appeals

A user who disputes a rejection, suspension or removal may appeal to
**[appeals contact]**. Review appeals impartially, ideally by a different
administrator than the one who took the original action, and respond within
**[e.g. 5 business days]**. Record the outcome.

## Records and consistency

Sensitive moderation actions are written to the audit log. Apply rules
consistently, keep notes proportionate and free of unnecessary personal data, and
review these procedures periodically.
