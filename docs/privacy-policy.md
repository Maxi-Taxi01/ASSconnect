# ASSconnect Privacy Policy

**Status: draft for review.** This policy describes how ASSconnect handles
personal data in server mode. It must be reviewed and approved for the actual
operating organisation, hosting setup and data-processing agreements before
public launch. It is not legal advice.

_Last updated: 19 June 2026._

## What data we collect

Account data: name, email address, password (stored only as a salted PBKDF2
hash), date of birth, role, and — where two-factor authentication is enabled — a
TOTP secret. We also keep email-verification and password-reset codes (hashed,
short-lived) and session records.

Student profile data: programme, study phase, study year, what you are looking
for, availability, location, remote preference, languages, skills, a short
biography, links, and — only with your consent — email, phone and an uploaded CV,
together with an optional profile photo and your visibility and consent choices.

Professional data: company name, website, sectors and description, and the
opportunities you post.

Interaction data: contact requests and conversation messages, saved-student
shortlists, reports, blocks, and privacy requests you submit.

Operational data: basic analytics events, an administrative audit log of
sensitive actions, a local email outbox in development, and database backups.

We do not intentionally collect special-category data. Please do not include
sensitive personal data (such as health or beliefs) in free-text fields.

## Why we use it and our legal basis

We process data to operate the matching service and to keep it secure. The legal
bases are: your **consent** for making your profile visible and for sharing your
contact details with professionals; **performance of a service / legitimate
interests** for operating accounts, search, messaging and moderation; and
**legal obligation / legitimate interests** for security, audit logging and abuse
prevention. A fuller breakdown is in the Records of Processing document.

## Visibility and sharing

Profiles require administrator approval before appearing in search. Contact
details and CV links are shown only when you have consented and the viewer is a
logged-in professional, an administrator, or you. We do not sell personal data.
Where production uses third-party processors (hosting, email, storage,
monitoring), they act under data-processing agreements.

## Email

Verification, password-reset, contact and notification emails are generated by
the service. In development these are written to a local outbox; in production
they are sent through a configured transactional email provider.

## Uploads

Profile photos and CVs are stored as files. In production, uploads move to private
object storage with malware scanning, and CV downloads are restricted to
authorised viewers.

## Your rights

You can access and export your data, correct it by editing your profile, and
delete your profile or account from the account tools. You can also request
restriction of or object to processing; a restriction hides your profile from
search until an administrator lifts it. Your export is provided in a portable
JSON format. To exercise any right, use the account tools or contact us; we
respond within the period required by law.

## Retention

We keep data only as long as needed. Specific periods are set out in the Data
Retention Schedule and include automated cleanup of expired sessions and tokens,
old analytics and stale unverified accounts.

## Security

Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2; sessions use server-issued bearer tokens with
expiry; verification and reset codes are hashed and short-lived; basic rate
limiting and login lockout are enforced; and sensitive actions are audit-logged.
Production additionally requires HTTPS, secure secret management, hardened session
storage, encrypted off-site backups, monitoring and a formal security review.

## Children

ASSconnect is not intended for anyone under 16, and registration enforces this
minimum age.

## Changes

We may update this policy and will communicate material changes through the
platform.
