## 1. About ASSconnect

ASSconnect ("the platform", "we", "us") is a matching service that connects
Applied Sciences students with professionals and organisations for internships,
graduation projects, research collaborations, career opportunities and jobs. The
service is operated by **[organisation name and legal entity]**, contactable at
**[contact email]**.

## 2. Who may use ASSconnect

You must be at least **16 years old** to create an account. Registration collects
a date of birth and rejects anyone below this age. Accounts are intended for
students, for professionals representing an organisation, and for administrators
appointed by the operator.

You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you provide and for
keeping your login credentials confidential. Two-factor authentication is
available and is recommended for professional and administrator accounts.

## 3. Accounts and verification

Creating an account requires a name, email address, password and date of birth.
You must verify your email address before logging in. We may suspend or close
accounts that breach these terms (see section 8).

## 4. Acceptable use

You agree not to: post false, misleading or unlawful content; impersonate another
person or organisation; upload malware or attempt to compromise the platform;
scrape, harvest or bulk-export other users' data; contact users for purposes
unrelated to the opportunities ASSconnect supports; or harass, abuse or
discriminate against others. Professionals must only use student contact details
that students have consented to share, and only to discuss genuine opportunities.

## 5. Content and moderation

Student profiles, company profiles and opportunities require administrator
approval before they appear publicly. We may decline, hide or remove content that
breaches these terms or the law. Users can report profiles and messages, and can
block other users from contacting them. Moderation is carried out as described in
the moderation procedures document.

## 6. Messaging

Professionals may send a first contact request to consenting, approved students.
Either party may reply within the resulting conversation. Recipients may block a
sender, after which that sender can no longer message them. We may review
messages that are reported for abuse.

## 7. Privacy and your data

Our handling of personal data is described in the Privacy Policy and the Records
of Processing document. You can access, correct, export, restrict, object to and
delete your data using the account tools or by contacting us. Restricting
processing hides your profile from search until the restriction is lifted.

## 8. Suspension and termination

We may suspend or permanently remove accounts that breach these terms, that are
used unlawfully, or that present a security or safety risk. You may delete your
own account at any time; deletion removes your profile from public view and
anonymises your account, while a limited audit record may be retained for
security and legal-compliance purposes as set out in the retention schedule.

## 9. Availability and limitations

ASSconnect is provided "as is" and "as available". We do not guarantee that any
opportunity will be found or that any match will result in an offer. We aim for
reliable operation but do not guarantee uninterrupted availability. ASSconnect
links to external opportunity sources (for example TNO, imec and TU Delft); those
sites are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.
